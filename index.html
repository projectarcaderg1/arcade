What is ARCADE?

ARCADE is an interactive learning platform that uses augmented reality and 3D models to help students explore science topics in a fun and engaging way.

How to Use

1. Tap Lessons
2. Choose a topic
3. Explore the 3D model or AR view
4. Listen and learn
5. Answer questions in the Activity section
