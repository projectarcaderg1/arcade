What is ARCADE?

ARCADE is an innovative augmented reality tool designed to enhance engagement and learning for Grade 9 students by combining 3D visualizations, interactive lessons, and quizzes.

How to Use ARCADE

  1. Scan the QR code on your flashcard to open the lesson page.
  2. Explore the 3D model, drag, rotate, and zoom it.
  3. Tap the Listen button to hear explanation.
  4. Click View in AR to see it in your real environment.
  5. Write your reflection and submit for points.
  6. Return to main page and click Activities to answer topic quizzes.
  7. Each correct answer gives 10 points. Total 80 points for 8 topics.
