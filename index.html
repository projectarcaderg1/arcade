✨ What is ARCADE?

ARCADE is an innovative educational platform that combines Augmented Reality, 3D visualization, and interactive feedback to enhance engagement and active learning among students.

🧭 How to Use

1️⃣ Open Lessons
2️⃣ Select a topic
3️⃣ Explore the 3D model
4️⃣ Tap View in AR
5️⃣ Press Listen for explanation
6️⃣ Submit reflection to earn points ⭐

⚠️ For faster AR loading:
Use Samsung Internet or Safari.
